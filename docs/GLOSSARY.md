# Stratos — Glossary

Canonical product terminology for Stratos. Use these definitions consistently across website copy, mobile app, documentation, and internal discussions.

---

## Koach

Stratos's AI coach. Koach provides personalized fitness and nutrition guidance through constructive, encouraging, and honest conversation. Koach remembers user goals, preferences, workout history, recovery patterns, and prior conversations over time *(depth of memory may vary by product phase — see MVP.md)*.

**Usage:** Always capitalize as **Koach**. Do not use "coach AI" or "the AI" in user-facing copy when referring to this feature by name.

---

## Journey Board

A community-facing surface where users share milestones, progress highlights, and training journeys. On the website, Journey Board content appears as curated highlights for inspiration and trust. In the mobile app, Journey Board supports fuller community interaction *(future / phased)*.

**Usage:** "Journey Board" — not "journey feed" or "progress board."

---

## Workout

A single training session consisting of exercises, sets, reps, weight, duration, or other prescribed work. A workout is logged when the user completes or records that session.

**Distinction:** A workout is one instance; a **routine** is the template or plan for workouts over time.

---

## Routine

A structured collection of workouts organized by schedule (e.g., weekly split). Routines are personalized to the user and adapt based on progress, recovery, and Koach recommendations.

**Usage:** "Your routine" or "weekly routine" — not interchangeable with **program** in all contexts, but related.

---

## Program

A longer-term training plan spanning weeks or months. A program may contain multiple **routines** or phases (e.g., hypertrophy block, deload week). Programs reflect periodization and goal alignment (fat loss, muscle building, general fitness).

**Distinction:** **Program** = macro plan; **routine** = meso/weekly structure; **workout** = single session.

---

## Recovery

The rest, sleep, mobility, and deload strategies recommended to the user based on training load, logged sessions, and self-reported feedback. Recovery guidance helps prevent overtraining and supports consistency.

**Usage:** "Recovery recommendations" — not limited to rest days; may include sleep, active recovery, and deload suggestions.

---

## Analytics

Data visualizations and insights derived from logged **workouts**, **sessions**, and progress over time. Examples: volume trends, strength progression, consistency streaks, and dashboard summaries.

**Scope:** Full **analytics** live in the mobile app. The website may reference analytics as a benefit but does not host the full dashboard.

---

## Progressive Overload

The gradual increase of training stress over time — typically through more weight, reps, sets, or intensity — to drive strength and muscle adaptation. Stratos tracks progressive overload to inform **routine** adjustments and **Koach** recommendations.

**Usage:** Use the full term in educational content; "progressive overload tracking" in feature lists.

---

## Wrapped

A periodic summary experience *(future / Could Have)* highlighting a user's training story over a defined period (e.g., month, quarter, year). Similar in spirit to year-in-review products — celebrates consistency, milestones, and personal bests.

**Usage:** "Stratos Wrapped" or "your Wrapped" — product name TBD *(assumption)*.

---

## Milestone

A meaningful achievement in the user's fitness journey. Examples: first completed week, personal record, consistency streak, goal weight, or program completion. Milestones may appear on the **Journey Board** and in **Wrapped** summaries.

---

## Session

A logged instance of user activity tied to a **workout**. A session records what was actually performed (sets, reps, weights, notes) versus what was prescribed. Sessions feed **analytics**, **progressive overload** tracking, and **Koach** adaptation.

**Distinction:** **Workout** = planned or prescribed; **session** = executed and logged.

---

## Onboarding

The web-based flow where new users provide goals, experience level, schedule, equipment, constraints, and preferences after **Sign Up**. Onboarding enables initial **program** / **routine** generation before the user continues in the mobile app.

---

## Dashboard

The primary in-app home for viewing upcoming **workouts**, recent **sessions**, progress summaries, and **Koach** access. Not hosted on the marketing website in full form.

---

## Community

The collective social layer of Stratos — shared **Journey Board** content, inspiration, and *(future)* peer interaction. Website community pages showcase highlights; full community features live in the mobile app.

---

## Premium

The paid subscription tier unlocking enhanced coaching, **analytics**, and personalization features beyond the free experience. Subscription management occurs primarily in the mobile app *(assumption)*.

---

## Adaptation

The ongoing process of adjusting **routines**, **programs**, recovery, and nutrition guidance based on logged **sessions**, user feedback, and **Koach** intelligence. Central to Stratos product philosophy — not one-time plan generation.

---

## Consistency

A core product outcome — the user's ability to maintain regular training and nutrition habits over time. Stratos optimizes for consistency over perfection in **Koach** messaging and feature design.

---

## Terms Not Used in Stratos Copy

| Avoid | Prefer |
|-------|--------|
| Bot, chatbot | Koach |
| Algorithm (user-facing) | Personalization, adaptation |
| Generate workouts (as sole value) | Adaptive coaching, personalized routines |
| Gym app (generic) | AI-powered adaptive fitness and nutrition coaching |

---

## Assumptions

- "Wrapped" naming and cadence are not finalized.
- Journey Board full mobile functionality is phased; glossary reflects intended end state.
- Program vs. routine distinction may simplify in UI copy but should remain precise in internal docs.
