# Stratos — Product Vision

## Mission

Help people build lasting fitness and nutrition habits through personalized, adaptive coaching that meets them where they are and grows with them over time.

## Vision

Stratos becomes the trusted AI coach in every lifter's pocket — one that remembers your history, understands your goals, and adapts your training and nutrition as your life and body change.

The mobile app is the primary experience. The website is the front door: it builds trust, explains the product, and gets users onboarded and into the app.

## Product Philosophy

Stratos is not a workout generator. It is a **continuous coaching relationship**.

Core beliefs:

1. **Adaptation over automation** — Plans should evolve based on performance, recovery, consistency, and feedback — not stay static after day one.
2. **Honesty with encouragement** — Koach tells the truth about progress, setbacks, and trade-offs while keeping the user motivated and supported.
3. **Consistency over perfection** — Small, repeatable wins matter more than ideal sessions that cannot be sustained.
4. **Memory creates trust** — Long-term recall of goals, preferences, injuries, and history makes coaching feel personal, not generic.
5. **Mobile-first execution** — Training happens in the gym, kitchen, and daily life. The app is where coaching is delivered; the website supports discovery and onboarding.

## Core Value Proposition

**For people who want structured fitness and nutrition guidance without generic plans or abandoned apps**, Stratos provides an AI coach that adapts workouts, recovery, and nutrition to their progress — and remembers them over time.

Users get:

- Personalized workout routines that adjust as they improve
- An AI coach (Koach) for guidance, motivation, and accountability
- Recovery and nutrition recommendations aligned with their training
- Workout logging and progressive overload tracking
- Dashboard and analytics to see real progress
- Community and Journey Board inspiration
- A path from signup on the web to full coaching in the mobile app

## What Makes Stratos Different

| Dimension | Typical fitness apps | Stratos |
|-----------|---------------------|---------|
| Plan creation | One-time program generation | Continuous adaptation to progress and recovery |
| Coaching tone | Generic tips or rigid scripts | Constructive, encouraging, honest AI coaching (Koach) |
| Memory | Session-only or none | Long-term memory of goals, history, and conversations |
| Primary experience | Often web-first or feature-heavy dashboards | Mobile-first coaching; website for trust and onboarding |
| Philosophy | "Here's a plan" | "Here's a coach that evolves with you" |

## Long-Term Goals

1. **Retention through adaptation** — Users stay because their plan improves with them, not because they need to restart every month.
2. **Trusted AI coaching** — Koach is recognized as a credible, supportive guide — not a novelty chatbot.
3. **Community as motivation** — Journey Board and community features reinforce consistency through shared progress.
4. **Sustainable monetization** — Premium tiers unlock deeper coaching, analytics, and personalization without compromising the core free experience.
5. **Platform coherence** — Website, mobile app, and backend share one product language and one user journey.

## Assumptions

- Users are willing to complete web onboarding before downloading the mobile app.
- Long-term AI memory is a meaningful differentiator for retention and perceived coaching quality.
- Subscription revenue will primarily come from mobile app users after they experience personalized coaching.
