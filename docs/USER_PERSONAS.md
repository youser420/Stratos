# Stratos — User Personas

Primary personas represent the audiences Stratos is designed to serve. Each persona may overlap with others (e.g., a beginner may also be focused on fat loss).

---

## 1. Beginner

**Profile:** New to structured training or returning after a long break. Limited confidence in the gym and unsure where to start.

**Goals**

- Learn proper exercise selection and progression
- Build a sustainable routine without overwhelm
- Feel guided rather than judged

**Pain Points**

- Too many apps offer generic plans that do not adapt
- Fear of doing exercises wrong or progressing too fast
- Difficulty staying consistent without accountability

**Motivations**

- Health, energy, and self-confidence
- Clear direction from a coach-like experience
- Visible progress even in small increments

**Expectations from Stratos**

- Simple onboarding that captures experience level and constraints
- Koach explains recommendations in accessible language
- Workouts that scale in difficulty as they improve
- Encouragement when consistency slips

---

## 2. Intermediate

**Profile:** Has trained consistently for 1–3 years. Understands basics but wants better structure, periodization, and recovery balance.

**Goals**

- Break plateaus with smarter programming
- Balance training volume, recovery, and nutrition
- Track progressive overload systematically

**Pain Points**

- Plateaus despite effort
- Programs that do not adjust when life disrupts training
- Fragmented tools (logging app + nutrition app + notes)

**Motivations**

- Measurable strength and physique improvements
- Efficiency — less guesswork, more intentional training
- A coach that remembers past cycles and preferences

**Expectations from Stratos**

- Adaptive routines based on logged performance
- Recovery recommendations tied to training load
- Analytics that show trends, not just daily logs
- Koach that gives honest feedback on deloads and adjustments

---

## 3. Advanced Lifter

**Profile:** Experienced trainee (3+ years). Trains with intent, understands periodization, and wants precision without hand-holding.

**Goals**

- Fine-tune volume, intensity, and exercise selection
- Maintain long-term progress while managing fatigue
- Leverage data for programming decisions

**Pain Points**

- Most apps oversimplify or prescribe beginner templates
- Generic AI advice that ignores training history
- Lack of continuity when switching programs or apps

**Motivations**

- Longevity in training — staying injury-free and progressing for years
- Data-driven coaching that respects their experience
- Tools that augment judgment, not replace it

**Expectations from Stratos**

- Koach that acknowledges their experience and avoids patronizing tone
- Progressive overload tracking with meaningful analytics
- Recovery guidance aligned with high training stress
- Memory of past blocks, injuries, and exercise preferences

---

## 4. Fat-Loss User

**Profile:** Primary goal is reducing body fat while preserving muscle and health. May be beginner to intermediate in training experience.

**Goals**

- Sustainable calorie deficit with adequate protein and training
- Consistent habits around food and movement
- Visible body composition changes over time

**Pain Points**

- Crash diets and unsustainable workout plans
- Conflicting advice on cardio vs. lifting
- Motivation drops when scale progress stalls

**Motivations**

- Looking and feeling better
- Health markers (energy, sleep, confidence)
- A coach that adjusts when progress slows

**Expectations from Stratos**

- Nutrition guidance aligned with training and recovery
- Workouts that support muscle retention during a cut
- Honest coaching when adherence or recovery is off track
- Journey Board and community for accountability

---

## 5. Muscle-Building User

**Profile:** Primary goal is hypertrophy and strength gain. Often intermediate; may overlap with advanced lifter persona.

**Goals**

- Progressive overload across key movement patterns
- Optimal volume and recovery for muscle growth
- Long-term muscle gain without unnecessary complexity

**Pain Points**

- Programs that stall or do not periodize volume
- Poor recovery leading to missed sessions
- Difficulty knowing when to push vs. deload

**Motivations**

- Strength and physique development
- Structured progression they can trust
- Coaching that adapts when gains slow

**Expectations from Stratos**

- Routines built around progressive overload
- Recovery recommendations when volume accumulates
- Analytics showing strength and volume trends
- Koach that recommends adjustments based on logged performance

---

## Persona Usage Notes

- **Website:** Messaging should speak to beginners (clarity, trust) and intermediates (adaptation, results) while not alienating advanced users (credibility, depth).
- **Onboarding:** Should capture goal type (fat loss, muscle building, general fitness), experience level, and constraints to route users into appropriate coaching.
- **Mobile app:** Primary persona fulfillment happens here — all personas expect Koach, logging, and adaptation in the app.

## Assumptions

- Most users will identify with one primary goal (fat loss or muscle building) even if secondary goals exist.
- Advanced lifters represent a smaller but high-engagement segment that influences brand credibility.
